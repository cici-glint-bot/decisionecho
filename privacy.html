DecisionEcho — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

DecisionEcho is an Atlassian Forge app developed by kaka tea dev ("we", "the developer"). This policy explains what data the app handles when you install and use it on your Atlassian site.

What the app stores

When a user records a decision through DecisionEcho, the app stores the decision record — title, conclusion, rationale, rejected alternatives, tags, linked issue keys, the author's display name, and the source URL the author provided — in Atlassian Forge app storage (Key-Value Store) associated with your site. This content is authored by your team; the app does not collect it from anywhere else.

What the app reads

To build a decision brief, the app reads, as the requesting user and within that user's existing permissions:

  • the summary, description and labels of the Jira issue being briefed;
  • the issue's recent comments and linked Atlassian Goals, via the Teamwork Graph API.

Reads are performed on demand to answer the request. Comment and goal content is used transiently for matching and is not persisted by the app.

What the app does not do

  • No data is sent to the developer or to any third-party server. The app runs entirely on Atlassian Forge infrastructure ("Runs on Atlassian") and has no external network egress.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or sale of data.
  • No storage of end-user personal data beyond the author display name attached to decision records a user deliberately creates.

Data retention and deletion

Decision records remain in your site's app storage until a user supersedes them or your admin uninstalls the app. Uninstalling the app removes its stored data in accordance with Atlassian Forge's app storage lifecycle.
